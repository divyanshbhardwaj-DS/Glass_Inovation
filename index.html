Knowledge & Standards

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about glass grades, safety compliance, installation timelines and warranties.

What is the difference between standard clear and Low-Iron glass?

Standard clear glass naturally possesses a subtle green tint caused by ferric oxide. Low-iron glass (such as Starphire) is formulated with significantly reduced iron content, delivering 99% optical clarity and 100% true-to-life colour fidelity. This is highly recommended for light-coloured splashbacks and ultra-premium shower screens.

What Australian Standards must frameless glass comply with?

All structural and architectural glass we fabricate strictly adheres to AS1288:2021 (Glass in Buildings - Selection and Installation) and AS1926.1 (Swimming Pool Safety). Every panel carries compliant certification and is thermal-toughened safety glass.

How fast can you attend for same-day glass repairs?

For urgent glass emergencies across Melbourne's inner west and western suburbs (Werribee, Footscray, Altona, Newport, Sunshine, etc.), our mobile response vehicle is typically on-site within 1–2 hours to make the perimeter safe and initiate same-day glass replacement.

How long does a custom quote and installation take?

Once you approve your on-site measure, custom toughened glass fabrication generally takes 5 to 7 business days. Our installation team then fits and silicone-seals the glass in a single seamless visit.
